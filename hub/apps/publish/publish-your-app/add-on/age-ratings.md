# Age ratings for add-ons

Age ratings are content suitability classifications that indicate the appropriate audience for your add-on. The International Age Rating Coalition (IARC) administers these ratings. During the add-on submission process, the **Age ratings** page lets you provide the rating information the Microsoft Store uses to show your content in supported markets. These ratings also determine whether your add-on needs rating information that differs from the parent product.

This article explains the age rating options available for add-ons so you can choose the option that matches your content.

## Age ratings options for add-ons

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You only need to provide age ratings information for add-ons that use the **Durable** product type.

When submitting a Durable add-on, select one of the following options on the **Age ratings** page:

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../msix/images/add-on-age-ratings.png" lightbox="../msix/images/add-on-age-ratings.png" alt-text="Screenshot of the Age ratings page for an add-on showing the options for receiving age ratings.":::

### This add-on does not require more restrictive ratings than its parent product

Select this option if the add-on contains content suitable for the same audience as the parent app or game and doesn't introduce content that would require a more restrictive age rating.

This option is the most common for add-ons such as:
* Cosmetic items
* Expansion content that aligns with the parent product's rating
* Additional features or functionality that don't change the content rating of the parent product

When you select this option, the add-on uses the parent product's rating information.

### I'm ready to complete the International Age Rating Coalition (IARC) questionnaire

Select this option if the add-on introduces content that may require a different or more restrictive age rating than the parent product. Partner Center prompts you to complete the IARC questionnaire. Answer all questions accurately so IARC can determine the appropriate age and content ratings for the add-on. After you complete the questionnaire, the Microsoft Store associates the generated rating information with the add-on and uses it in supported markets.

### I have already completed the questionnaire elsewhere and have an IARC Certificate ID or Global Rating ID (GRID)

Select this option if age ratings for the add-on already exist from another storefront or rating process and you have an existing IARC Certificate ID or Global Rating ID (GRID). Enter the existing rating identifier to associate those ratings with the add-on.

## Age rating updates

If an update introduces content that could affect the age rating of the add-on, review the rating information and complete the IARC questionnaire again if necessary. You are responsible for ensuring that rating information remains accurate and reflects the content available in the add-on.
